Pharmaceutical Microbiology syllabus (BP303T), unit-wise

  1. Unit 1 · Introduction, bacteria, culture and microscopy

    History, branches and scope of microbiology; prokaryotes vs eukaryotes; ultrastructure and morphological classification of bacteria; nutritional needs, culture media and growth conditions; growth curve; isolation and preservation of pure cultures; cultivation of anaerobes; total and viable counts. Phase contrast, dark field and electron microscopy.

  2. Unit 2 · Staining, IMViC and sterilization

    Simple, Gram's and acid-fast staining; IMViC tests. Physical, chemical and mechanical sterilization: principle, procedure, merits, demerits, applications. Evaluating sterilization efficiency, large-scale sterilization equipment, sterility indicators.

  3. Unit 3 · Fungi, viruses, disinfectants and sterility testing

    Morphology, classification, reproduction/replication and cultivation of fungi and viruses. Classification and mode of action of disinfectants; factors influencing disinfection; evaluation of antiseptics (bactericidal and bacteriostatic). Sterility testing of products as per IP, BP and USP.

  4. Unit 4 · Aseptic area and microbiological assay

    Design of an aseptic area, laminar air flow, sources of contamination and their prevention, clean area classification. Principles and methods of microbiological assay; standardization of antibiotics, vitamins and amino acids; assessment of a new antibiotic.

  5. Unit 5 · Spoilage, preservation and cell culture

    Types of spoilage, factors affecting microbial spoilage, sources and types of contaminants, assessment of spoilage. Preservation with antimicrobial agents and evaluation of microbial stability. Animal cell culture: general procedure, primary, established and transformed cell lines, applications in industry and research.

Pharmaceutical Microbiology important questions

Questions in the university exam pattern for BP303T, grouped by unit. Practise writing them; model answers with key-point checking open with the ₹49 plan.

Unit 1: Introduction, bacteria, culture and microscopy

  • [10 marks] Describe the ultrastructure of a bacterial cell with a neat labelled diagram.

Unit 2: Staining, IMViC and sterilization

  • [10 marks] Describe the principle, construction, working, merits, demerits and applications of moist heat sterilization by autoclave. How is its efficiency evaluated?

Unit 3: Fungi, viruses, disinfectants and sterility testing

  • [10 marks] Describe the morphology, classification, reproduction and cultivation of fungi. Add a note on their pharmaceutical importance.

Unit 4: Aseptic area and microbiological assay

  • [10 marks] Design an aseptic area for sterile injectables, justifying the layout, air handling and contamination control measures.

Unit 5: Spoilage, preservation and cell culture

  • [10 marks] A solid dosage manufacturing area reports recurrent fungal contamination. Discuss the probable sources, investigation and corrective and preventive measures.
